# HOLDFAST — characters

> Two principals. A four-minute film cannot hold more and does not need to.
> Names are **draft** pending canon-keeper approval per `CANON_LOCKED.md` § naming
> ("new entities must have Arcanean-quality names").

## KESS ANDAL — apprentice, House Terra, rank 0

**Age** 19. **On paper** the Academy's longest-serving failure: eleven terms,
zero Gates.

### Want
To be allowed to stop. Not to succeed — to be *released*. She has been asking
for eleven terms and every adult has heard it as laziness.

*(Note the difference from the plot want. The plot want is "pass the
assessment." The human want is "let me put it down." The film is about the
second one and never says it.)*

### Wound
She was six. She doesn't remember agreeing to anything. She remembers the
afternoon it started and nothing about the afternoon before.

### What she will not say
That she is tired. She has an entire vocabulary built to route around the word.
She will say a wall is cold, that the stairs are long, that the light is bad.
She will never say *tired*, and when someone else says it about her she corrects
them to something physical.

### Physical asymmetry
The last two fingers of her left hand don't fully straighten — held in the same
position too long, too young. She never offers the hand, and never lets Vesk
take it, until **beat 9**, where she does.

*(This read "until 3:12" until review caught it. 3:12 is the start of beat 12,
which is Vesk's hand on the door — not hers. Stale ordering from an earlier
draft, and a violation of this file's own rule at the foot of the page: **refer
to beats, not timecodes.** The rule was written after fixing one instance of
exactly this and did not catch the second one in the same file.)*

**Open for stage 6:** "she hides the hand" is not quite what the documents
describe. Beat 1 is a close, low shot of her hands setting a stone, so the curl
is plausibly on screen in the film's first frames — unremarked, unreadable, and
only meaningful in retrospect at beat 9. That is the stronger version and it is
not the same as hiding. The shot list decides whether the curl is visible in
beat 1; whatever it decides, this section follows it rather than the reverse.

### Word Ledger

**Only she says:** *bed* (as a mason's term — the face a stone sits on) ·
*course* · *spall* · *green* (freshly quarried stone, still wet) · *plumb*

**She cannot say:** *tired* · *help* · *please* · *scared* · *mine*

**Shared, by design:** *hold* — the film's one deliberate collision, and
therefore **not** in the exclusive list above. See § Word Ledger conflicts.

**Grammatical tic:** She answers questions with a physical fact about the room.
*"Are you sleeping?" — "The east wall sweats after rain."* Never evasive in tone,
always evasive in content. It reads as odd for ninety seconds, as a symptom at
two minutes, and as the most honest thing in the film after the turn.

### Locked visual string
> A nineteen-year-old woman, House Terra apprentice. Short dark hair, cut by
> herself, uneven at the back. Broad through the shoulders and forearms from
> stone work, thin everywhere else. Grey-green work canvas over a rough
> undershirt, sleeves pushed to the elbow. Stone dust in the creases of her
> knuckles and along the forearms. Deep shadow under both eyes, permanent, not
> makeup. Chapped lower lip. Left hand: last two fingers curled, will not
> straighten. Never smiles. Watches the floor when spoken to.

### Arc
Not *weak → strong*. **Unseen → seen → released.** She does not gain power in
this film. Someone finally counts what she is already doing, and she is allowed
to set it down. That is the whole emotional transaction, and it is enough.

## SENTINEL ORIN VESK — assessor, Starlight Corps, five-point

**Age** 50s. Sent to close her file. He has done this eleven hundred times and
is good at it in the way that a person becomes good at something they should
have stopped doing.

### Want
To finish the assessment before the weather turns. He has four more yards after
this one. He is not cruel — he is *behind*.

### Wound
He was measured out of something once, by a number, and has spent thirty years
on the side of the instrument. He never says this. It is in one line about his
own hands.

### What he will not say
That the instrument is wrong. He has suspected it for a decade and has a
pension.

### Physical asymmetry
A badly set break in the bridge of his nose. Old. His left eye waters in cold
and he wipes it with the back of his wrist, constantly, an unconscious tic that
reads first as dismissiveness and later — after we understand what he's looking
at — as something else.

### Word Ledger

**Only he says:** *threshold* · *variance* · *log* · *terminal* (as in a
terminal reading) · *file*

**He cannot say:** *sorry* · *wrong* · *stay* · *girl* · *enough*

**Grammatical tic:** He speaks in the passive when he is delivering bad news and
in the active when he is curious. The switch is the tell that he has stopped
assessing and started looking, and it happens once, opening **beat 9** — one
beat before the turn. *"The file is closed at the end of term"* → *"Show me your
hand."*

### Locked visual string
> A man in his fifties, Starlight Corps sentinel, five-point star insignia at
> the collar. Grey stubble, close-cropped grey hair receding at the temples.
> Badly set break across the bridge of the nose. Left eye wet, wiped often with
> the back of the wrist. Starlight-silver coat over dark field uniform, worn at
> the cuffs, immaculately kept. Carries a brass-and-crystal measuring instrument
> on a strap. Reading glasses he puts on only to write.

### Function
He is the audience. He arrives believing what they believe, sees it before they
do, and his face at **beat 10** — the turn — is where the film's emotion
actually lands. She has
known this her whole life. He is the one who finds out.

## KAELITH — the Godbeast, Foundation

Never seen whole. Never named aloud.

Present only as: pressure in the sub-bass · dust that falls upward for two
frames in **beat 5** · the weight in the door · and in **beat 13**, in the
seam-light, one shape too large to resolve, held for eleven frames and never
repeated.

**Rule: no wide shot of Kaelith exists in this film.** The single most valuable
thing a pilot can do with a canon's biggest asset is withhold it. Everyone will
show their monster. We show the dust it moves.

## Word Ledger conflicts — checked

**Exactly one shared word, and it is deliberate.** Everything else is disjoint:
his register is institutional and abstract (*threshold, variance, file*), hers
physical and trade-specific (*bed, course, spall, plumb*). Their two
vocabularies cannot describe the same object, which is the argument of the film
rendered in word choice.

The shared word is **"hold"** — his in the institutional sense at **beat 4**
(*"Hold still,"* over the instrument), hers in the literal sense at **beat 12**.
Same word, and only one of them knows what it costs. That collision is the
script's strongest line and neither character comments on it.

Because it is shared, `hold` is **excluded from either exclusive list** —
listing it under "only she says" would make these two sections contradict each
other, and the exclusive lists are what a drafting pass at stage 4 reads first.
Kess's fifth exclusive word is *plumb*.

*(Free, unplanned, and worth keeping: Vesk's institutional word **thres-hold**
literally contains hers. Nobody points at it.)*

*Referenced by beat, not timecode, deliberately: dialogue is not locked until
stage 4, so any timecode quoted here would go stale the moment a line moves.
Beats are stable; the seconds inside them are not.*
