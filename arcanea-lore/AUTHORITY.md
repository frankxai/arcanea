## Status of this directory

`arcanea-lore/` contains valuable historical worldbuilding, knowledge-base syntheses, planning documents, and earlier canon attempts. Preserve them as project history and reusable source material.

They do **not** supersede `.arcanea/lore/CANON_LOCKED.md`.

In particular, `arcanea-lore/CANON.md` is a legacy master-prompt/knowledge-base synthesis. Statements within it such as:

`GOD — THE SOURCE — THE TAO`

are preserved as earlier ideation, **not current canonical theological equivalence**.

## Conflict rule

When a legacy file conflicts with `.arcanea/lore/CANON_LOCKED.md`:

1. preserve the legacy file/history;
2. follow `CANON_LOCKED.md` for current fiction;
3. record valuable contradictions in a migration/conflict ledger;
4. move improvements through STAGING and reviewed canon changes rather than silently rewriting history.

## Real-world religion and Starlight boundary

Arcanea is fiction.

Real religious, philosophical, literary, and mythological traditions may inspire original Arcanean worldbuilding, but Arcanea does not claim to reveal what those traditions “really meant.”

`Starlight Intelligence` is reserved for the real-world Starlight institution/philosophy, not an Arcanean mystical force. The proposed fictional in-world replacement for that former STAGING concept is `Starweave Attunement`.
