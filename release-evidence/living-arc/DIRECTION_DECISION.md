# The Living Arc — Visual Direction Decision

> **Decision date:** 2026-08-04
>
> **Primary direction:** 01 — Cinematic Codex
>
> **Evidence:** `selected-unwritten-gate.png`, `directions/02-vertical-memory-fracture.jpg`, `directions/03-living-archive.jpg`

## Decision criteria

The flagship experience must:

1. make the moral hook legible before lore;
2. feel continuous with Arcanea’s obsidian, gold, teal, and natural-material identity;
3. support long-form reading without turning the story into a product dashboard;
4. adapt into webtoon, wallpaper, and social crops without creating separate brands;
5. protect visible focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, and narrow-screen composition.

## Exactly three explored directions

### 01 — Cinematic Codex — selected

Full-bleed character and world art, editorial serif headlines, restrained mono metadata, and one meaningful interaction at the threshold.

**Why it wins:** the people and the dilemma arrive together. It preserves Arcanea’s premium visual identity while giving the prose enough quiet space to carry nuance. The hero art survives 16:9, 4:5, and 9:16 adaptations.

**Risk:** visual density can compete with copy. The implementation answers this with strong ink gradients, limited line lengths, and moonbone reading sections.

### 02 — Vertical Memory Fracture — reserved for the webtoon

Uninterrupted portrait panels, large silent gutters, and text treated as a consequence in space.

**Why it is not the whole flagship:** its intensity is ideal for Episode 00 but exhausting for reference material, the series map, and downloadable assets. Navigation becomes fragile when every transition is cinematic.

**Where it survives:** `/story/the-living-arc/webtoon/episode-0` and short-form video cards.

### 03 — Living Archive — reserved for codex and intelligence surfaces

A moonbone editorial ledger beside luminous art, with facts, sources, Echoes, and revision history organized as records.

**Why it is not the launch direction:** it makes Arcanea feel intelligent but places the system before the wound. The initial encounter needs attachment to Malachar, Lumara, and Elyon before it needs an encyclopedia interface.

**Where it survives:** future lore records, artifact ledgers, spirit archetype pages, and Starlight memory tools.

## Rejected generic recommendation

An automated design-system search proposed an Orbitron/JetBrains cyberpunk dashboard with a pink CTA. That direction was rejected because it was optimized for product software rather than mythic narrative, introduced an unrelated accent color, and weakened Arcanea’s established material language.

## Locked visual grammar

- **Ink:** `#05070d`
- **Obsidian:** `#071821`
- **Moonbone:** `#f1e9d7`
- **Peacock cyan:** `#62f4de`
- **Antique gold:** `#d6b468`
- **Display:** Cinzel / classical serif fallback
- **Reading:** Crimson Pro / literary serif fallback
- **Metadata:** JetBrains Mono / technical mono fallback
- **Image rule:** one dominant symbolic idea per image
- **Motion rule:** motion clarifies choice, progress, or state; reduced motion keeps the complete experience
- **Originality rule:** elemental archetypes may be ancient and shared; names, costs, relationships, silhouettes, materials, and story consequences must remain Arcanea-native

## Release implication

Direction 01 governs the flagship route, wallpapers, and carousel. Direction 02 governs the vertical episode and Reel. Direction 03 becomes the design seed for the future searchable encyclopedia. This creates one coherent family without forcing every medium into one layout.
