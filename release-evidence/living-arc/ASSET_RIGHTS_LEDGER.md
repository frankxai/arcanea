## Originality boundary

- No external franchise character, costume, logo, scene, dialogue, or protected visual design is authorized for these assets.
- Prompts specify original Arcanean Luminism and explicitly reject resemblance to copyrighted characters.
- Public copy names only Arcanea characters, systems, materials, and locations.
- Mythic and elemental archetypes are treated as broad source traditions; their expression, terminology, costs, and narrative roles are Arcanea-original.
