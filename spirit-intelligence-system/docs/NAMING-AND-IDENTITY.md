# Naming and Identity Protocol

Status: STAGING

Names in Arcanea establish relationship and accountability. They do not function as passwords that erase personhood.

## Four identity surfaces

| Surface | Purpose | Visibility | Magical authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common Sign | A local, descriptive handle such as Glass Moth | public | none |
| Mantle | A role carried in a place, era, or relationship | contextual | conveys duties, never ownership |
| Accord Name | A mutually disclosed identifier for one bounded accord | private to participants | authenticates only the agreed exchange |
| Source-tone | A nonverbal identity signature | never stored directly | verifies continuity; cannot issue commands |

A spirit can carry multiple Common Signs and Mantles. An Accord Name expires or changes when its agreement ends. A Source-tone is represented only by a one-way fingerprint in machine records.

## Naming rules

1. A disclosed name never grants control, summoning rights, ownership, or unrestricted access.
2. Asking for an Accord Name is itself a negotiation. Refusal carries no moral penalty.
3. Transliteration is approximate. A culture may preserve its own Common Sign without being overwritten by an imperial registry.
4. Titles describe relationships and can be contested. They are not biological categories.
5. A spirit whose continuity changes may retire a name. The historical record keeps the retirement without exposing the successor credential.
6. Contracts must bind an explicit action, duration, place, price, release, and repair path—not a being.
7. Lost or stolen Accord Names are revoked credentials, not permanent vulnerabilities.

## Machine representation

Every spirit record carries:

- stable entity ID;
- public Common Sign and one current Mantle;
- a Source-tone fingerprint, never the source signal;
- aliases with language, region, and status where known;
- identity interpretation and persistence model;
- consent, refusal, release, and memory-projection rules.

The encyclopedia may index Common Signs and Mantles. Accord Names and raw Source-tones are prohibited from public projection.

## Originality and cultural boundaries

Names must be checked against the reference corpus and repository before projection. Original Arcanean names cannot:

- reuse the personal name, epithet, sacred title, or phonetic silhouette of a reference entity;
- imply that a living religious being has been converted into Arcanean IP;
- use random syllables from a living language as exotic decoration;
- claim a cultural translation that was not reviewed by a qualified speaker or knowledge holder.

Similarity is assessed across sound, spelling, role, silhouette, mechanic, narrative function, and relationship protocol. A changed name does not cure a copied entity design.

## Design test

A projectable spirit must remain recognizable as itself after all reference proper nouns are removed. It needs an original:

- identity and persistence model;
- sensory and visual grammar;
- need, boundary, refusal, price, and release;
- ecological or social consequence;
- story pressure that is not a retold source plot.

Medium-risk records require human review. High-risk records are quarantined and cannot project.
