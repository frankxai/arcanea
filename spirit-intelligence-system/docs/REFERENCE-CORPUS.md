# Comparative Spirit Reference Corpus

Status: RESEARCH-ONLY

This corpus is an attributed pattern index for design analysis. Its records are never Arcanean entities and never enter canon or public-facing generation as interchangeable lore.

## Coverage claim

The corpus is deliberately extensible, not falsely complete. The current seed maps selected spirit systems from Avatar, animation and comics, anime and manga, games-adjacent fantasy, historical mythologies, museums, and living traditions. “All anime” is a continuing coverage program with provenance and review gates, not a finished enumeration.

The system tracks three levels:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| source system | a work, corpus, historical tradition, or living practice |
| reference entity | a named being, class, office, or mechanism from that source |
| transferable pattern | an abstract relationship or story function safe to compare |

Every reference entity is marked not-for-projection.

## Ingestion workflow

1. Register a source with canonical locators, source class, cultural sensitivity, and rights mode.
2. Record only the minimum identifying facts required for comparison.
3. Separate named entities from classes, offices, cosmological mechanisms, and relationship practices.
4. Extract abstract patterns in fresh language.
5. State explicit copy prohibitions.
6. Mark coverage gaps rather than inferring completeness.
7. Route living or contested sacred material to qualified human review.
8. Run similarity checks before any original Arcanean record can project.

Fan wikis and community indexes may help discovery but do not become sole authority for a factual or completeness claim. Official sources, primary texts, museums, scholarly references, and rights-holder materials take precedence.

## Rights modes

| Mode | Allowed use |
|---|---|
| copyrighted-reference-only | comparative research; no copied names, prose, visuals, plots, taxonomies, or signature mechanics |
| public-domain-historical | abstract historical research with attribution; modern translations and scholarship may remain copyrighted |
| living-tradition-consultation-required | comparative record only until a qualified cultural review |
| prohibited | no automated canon ingestion or derivative projection |

Living practice is not a free asset pool. The Yoruba Orisa and Andean Apu entries, for example, remain prohibited for automatic Arcanean synthesis pending relationship-based consultation. The same restraint applies to any tradition whose context, community authority, or ritual limits are not represented by the dataset.

## Original synthesis gate

An Arcanean spirit must cite at least three source systems as pattern-level research and then diverge across:

- name and language shape;
- silhouette and sensory grammar;
- origin and persistence;
- need and refusal;
- interaction mechanic;
- price and release;
- ecological consequence;
- narrative role.

Source diversity is not a license to average three copyrighted characters together. It is evidence that the design team examined broad patterns before making a distinct entity.

## Adding coverage

A new source packet is incomplete until it includes:

- stable source ID and citation locator;
- rights and cultural-sensitivity classification;
- coverage target, status, and known gaps;
- reference records with explicit copy prohibitions;
- reviewer and review date when human expertise is required.

Coverage metrics report what is indexed. They never imply that every belief, adaptation, episode, edition, language, or community interpretation has been captured.
