# Arcanea — A world you enter. A world you help create.

**Status:** 2026-11-04 commercial and narrative proposal. Not approved canon, current storefront, legal entity claim or deployed release. Arcanea locked fictional canon and rights are distinct from real-world research, SIS/ACOS operations and FrankX founder copy. [Portfolio source in review](https://github.com/frankxai/agentic-ops/pull/471).

## Promise and experience
The Arcanea world is authored, immersive, coherent and participatory. Its goal isn't a pile of generated images; it's worlds, music and adventures people return to—and creative work they can proudly own and carry forward.

**Invitation for editorial review:** "Some worlds wait to be discovered. Arcanea asks what you'll create inside them."

Choose **one** original World Seed or Music Mentor piano slice with inspected mobile/desktop, human listening and rights; keep source/canon lineage, composition/master ownership, alt text, controlled sound, reduced motion, rights/attribution and customer export. A clean sample must work before adding paid marketplace listings. No checkout, price, new canon or live release without the exact registered owner, independent review, support/refund and human approval.

Arcanea Records is an existing registered-trade-name assertion in historical strategy, **not** evidence of a separately incorporated record label. Streaming is discovery; direct editions and licensing deserve their own economics. Reuse Starlight shared billing/identity only where authorized; never duplicate platform rails.
