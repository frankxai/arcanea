# Influence & Rights Ledger

> **Purpose:** extract transferable design principles without importing protected expression.
> **Reviewed:** 2026-08-03

## Operating rule

Arcanea studies mechanisms, not costumes. Titles, names, visual designs, distinctive object forms, exact counts, power mappings, phrases, histories, and setting-specific logic remain with their source.

No entry below authorizes copying. The ledger records why a source was studied, what narrow principle may transfer, how Arcanea transforms it, and what is excluded.

## Mythological and historical traditions

These sources are not treated as a free pool of fantasy nouns. Public-domain stories can coexist with living religious practice, cultural ownership, modern translations, and community-specific meaning. Arcanea extracts abstract functions only; direct cultural representation requires specialist review.

## Expansion queue

The ledger should next commission source packets—not aesthetic scraping—for Mesopotamian authority tablets and seals; Hindu and Buddhist invoked weapons and vajra traditions; Yoruba orisha-linked objects; Chinese ritual seals and swords; Mesoamerican regalia; Polynesian genealogical objects; Indigenous North American sacred bundles; Islamic talismanic and calligraphic traditions; and Sámi ceremonial objects.

Each packet needs a culturally competent researcher, primary or museum-grade sources, living-practice sensitivity, translation and image rights, internal diversity, and an explicit answer to whether Arcanea should reference the tradition at all.

## Approval test

Before an influence ID may appear in an approved artifact:

1. the source is exact and its rights status is recorded;
2. only an abstract design primitive transfers;
3. protected or sacred expression is named in `prohibitedParallels`;
4. at least two independently Arcanean mechanics dominate the artifact;
5. the artifact still makes sense when the source title is removed from the design discussion;
6. a human reviewer clears the similarity and cultural-risk decision.
