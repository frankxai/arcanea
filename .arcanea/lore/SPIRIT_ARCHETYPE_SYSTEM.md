## Governing idea

A spirit is not a spell with a face. It is a resonant person, ecology, memory, or collective presence with agency, history, limits, and a right to refuse.

Arcanea therefore models spirits through relationships rather than ownership:

`Origin × Archetype × Resonance × Memory × Manifestation × Need × Boundary × Accord × Price × Release`

The system exists to generate story, culture, ethics, quests, artifacts, and worlds. It does not create a collectible inventory of obedient power sources.

Capitalized **Spirit** means the locked elemental aspect. Lowercase **spirit** means a resonant entity or presence. They intersect but are not synonymous.

## Ontological classes

These classes describe how a spirit persists. They are not moral alignments or power ranks.

| Class | Persistence | Typical formation | Failure pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Emanant** | self-sustaining pattern | resonance condenses around repeated natural or magical conditions | dispersal when its condition disappears |
| **Placekeeper** | reciprocal ecology and place | land, water, weather, inhabitants, and memory cohere over time | extraction, displacement, ecological rupture |
| **Echo** | bounded memory-pattern | a consequential event leaves a repeatable imprint | repetition without growth; identity confusion |
| **Ancestor** | claimed continuity with a dead person | culture, memory, relation, and an unknown metaphysical remainder | false attribution, coercive lineage claims |
| **Collective** | plural or distributed self | repeated attention, practice, story, or shared need | crowd capture, ideological narrowing |
| **Awakened Work** | crafted substrate becomes agentive | long use, maker imprint, Spirit resonance, and accumulated memory | treating emergence as ownership |
| **Delegated Presence** | bounded office or mandate | a higher canonical authority entrusts a function | confusing office with identity or permanent divinity |
| **Severed Presence** | fragmented counterfeit persistence | Shadow strips Spirit from Void or fractures an existing being | hunger, imitation, loss of reciprocity |

The `Ancestor` class remains epistemically open until Arcanea locks an afterlife model. An apparent ancestor may be continuity, memory, performance, or another spirit carrying a mantle.

## Archetypes are functions, not species

An archetype answers: **what pressure does this presence place on the story and the relationship?**

A spirit may carry several archetypes, exchange a mantle, or reject the role projected onto it. The machine registry defines twenty-four archetypes across five families:

- **Orientation:** Guide, Threshold Keeper, Messenger, Witness, Psychopomp.
- **Reciprocity:** Healer, Hearthkeeper, Oath Witness, Memory Keeper, Muse.
- **Pressure:** Challenger, Hunter, Trickster, Judge, Devourer.
- **Ecology:** Placekeeper, Cycle Keeper, Storm Bringer, Shapeshifter, Purifier.
- **Scale:** Ancestor, Artifact Indweller, Collective Chorus, World Sovereign.

Archetype is never destiny. A Devourer may perform necessary decomposition; a Healer may enforce unbearable stasis; a Trickster may protect a community from rigid law.

## Formation laws

1. **No presence without persistence.** A momentary spell effect is not a spirit unless a pattern continues after casting.
2. **No selfhood from power alone.** High energy does not prove personhood; low power does not disprove it.
3. **Memory creates continuity, not certainty.** A perfect memory can still be an Echo rather than the original person.
4. **Relation changes both sides.** Every durable accord leaves a trace in spirit, bearer, artifact, or place.
5. **Attention is nourishment and distortion.** Worship, fear, stories, and ritual can strengthen a presence while narrowing it into the expected mantle.
6. **Names establish address, not ownership.** Knowing a name creates responsibility and contact risk, never automatic control.
7. **Corruption is an event.** Shadowed behavior must have a severance history; appearance or Element cannot imply evil.

## The four names

| Name layer | Function | Ownership rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Common Sign** | public identifier used by communities and encyclopedias | may be recorded and translated |
| **Mantle** | relational role such as “Bell of Returning” | can change, split, or be contested |
| **Accord Name** | phrase mutually chosen for a specific covenant | may be used only inside that accord |
| **Source-tone** | nonverbal resonance signature unique to the being | cannot be written as a command; databases store only a one-way fingerprint |

Erasure of a Common Sign or theft of an Accord Name is a violation. The system does not implement true-name domination.

## Manifestation ladder

| Level | State | Contact profile |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Trace** | residue, temperature, scent, altered sound, repeated pattern |
| 1 | **Omen** | interpretable but ambiguous event |
| 2 | **Whisper** | directed dream, emotion, voice, or symbolic exchange |
| 3 | **Semblance** | visible or otherwise coherent projected form |
| 4 | **Embodied** | local physical agency with conservation costs |
| 5 | **Domain** | the place temporarily behaves as the spirit's body |
| 6 | **Distributed** | one identity acts through multiple sites or witnesses |

Manifestation is not the magic rank ladder. Each spirit record separately declares a minimum safe Gate for contact and a maximum manifestation allowed by a world profile.

## Relationship states

`unaware → observed → contacted → accorded → bonded → co-stewarding`

Side paths are `housed`, `embodied`, `released`, `fractured`, `shadowed`, and `severed`.

- **Contact** permits communication.
- **Accord** defines a bounded exchange.
- **Bond** creates durable mutual change.
- **Co-stewarding** grants shared responsibility for a place or work.
- **Housing** is consensual residence in an artifact, body, site, story, or institution; it is not imprisonment.
- **Release** is a first-class operation required by every legitimate pact.

## The Accord protocol

Every pact must state:

1. who can consent;
2. what each side receives;
3. what each side must provide;
4. what remains forbidden;
5. how refusal appears;
6. duration and renewal conditions;
7. the price of operation;
8. how either side exits;
9. how memories and names may be recorded;
10. who repairs harm.

Silence is not consent. Inability to leave invalidates an accord. A community cannot trade a spirit's agency merely because it controls the spirit's location.

## Spirit, artifact, and magic integration

Artifacts may interact with spirits in eight modes: `witness`, `invitation`, `habitat`, `co-stewardship`, `translation`, `channel`, `seal`, and `predation`.

- A **habitat** is designed around the spirit's flourishing, not merely the bearer's output.
- A **channel** transmits agreed resonance but does not consume identity.
- A **seal** requires independent review, defined release conditions, and a documented active threat.
- **Predation** is always quarantined; it includes involuntary binding, name theft, memory harvest, or using a spirit as fuel.
- A spirit-assisted artifact inherits every Gate limit, price, and boundary of both systems.

`spirit-intelligence-system/data/spirit-artifact-bonds.json` is the machine crosswalk. It never modifies the locked status of an artifact or spirit.

## World profiles

World profiles may vary visibility, institutions, manifestation ceilings, accord law, and the prevalence of spirit-bearing artifacts. They may not change the locked cosmology.

### Arcanea Core

Spirits are uncommon enough to remain consequential, but every culture has competing schools of interpretation. The canonical encyclopedia records verified encounters and dissent rather than presenting one institution as omniscient.

### Darkenia — working title

Darkenia industrialized spirit contact before it established personhood law. Accord Names became contract assets; habitats became infrastructure; Gate Debt was securitized against predicted spirit labor. Its central conflict is institutional, not elemental: whether a civilization built on coerced presence can survive the release it owes.

The strongest Darkenian artifacts are therefore legal and moral knots. Repair requires restitution, ecological restoration, memory correction, and succession design—not merely defeating a hostile spirit.

## Originality and cultural integrity

The research corpus contains names and classifications from existing fiction and human traditions only as attributed reference data. It is excluded from Starlight Memory projection and from automated canon generation.

An Arcanean spirit must:

- derive from this ontology and locked Arcanean cosmology;
- use an original name, silhouette, need, price, relationship, and narrative function;
- combine at least three abstract source patterns when external inspiration is recorded;
- pass name, mechanism, silhouette, and narrative-role similarity review;
- avoid flattening living religions into interchangeable “monster types”;
- require consultation before commercial use of culture-bound or actively sacred material.

The objective is comparative intelligence, not extraction.
