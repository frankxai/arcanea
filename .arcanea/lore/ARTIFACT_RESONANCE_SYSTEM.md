# ARCANEA ARTIFACT & RESONANCE SYSTEM

> **Status: STAGING** — additive canon proposal; requires Frank's explicit approval before locking.
> **Date:** 2026-08-03
> **Authority:** `.arcanea/lore/CANON_LOCKED.md` and `.arcanea/lore/MAGIC_SYSTEM.md` remain upstream and unchanged.

## Decision

Arcanea does not need a parallel magic system. It needs a compositional layer between the locked magic grammar and the objects, worlds, economies, quests, and communities that make magic inhabitable.

The proposed layer has four primitives:

1. **Expressions** — derived forms composed from the locked Elements.
2. **Artifacts** — bounded interfaces to one or more magical techniques.
3. **Assemblies** — compatible artifacts combined into a larger working.
4. **World Profiles** — local ecologies that determine how the same cosmic laws manifest in different worlds.

The governing invariant is:

> **An artifact may grant access to a capability. It never grants rank, opened Gates, judgment, culture, or mastery.**

This makes relic-mediated power narratively dangerous without dissolving the Ten Gates.

## Originality doctrine

Arcanea may study the deep structures behind fantasy and myth: elemental affinity, semantic casting, bonded tools, divine gifts, dangerous regalia, distributed relic sets, maker imprint, sovereignty, sacrifice, and corruption.

Arcanea does not import signature expression. Forbidden imports include franchise titles, proper nouns, terminology, visual silhouettes, exact counts, color-to-power mappings, incantations, histories, cosmologies, or distinctive artifact behavior. Every external influence is recorded in the influence and rights ledger before it reaches STAGING.

The transformation threshold is structural:

- no single source supplies more than one defining primitive;
- every artifact derives mechanically from Arcanea's Gates, tiers, disciplines, Elements, materials, and cosmology;
- every artifact carries an Arcanea-specific price, counterplay, provenance, and historical consequence;
- high similarity risk cannot advance beyond STAGING without redesign.

## The resonance stack

Magic moves through six layers:

```text
Lumina / Nero
  -> Element
  -> Expression
  -> Technique
  -> Interface
  -> Consequence
```

- **Element** — Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Void, or Spirit; locked.
- **Expression** — an emergent behavior produced by one or more Elements.
- **Technique** — a bounded effect classifiable by discipline, tier, Gate, and rank.
- **Interface** — body, language, pact, location, ritual, or artifact through which the technique is invoked.
- **Consequence** — the energetic, personal, social, or ecological price that makes power causal.

An Expression is not a new Element. A culture may give it a local name, but its component Elements remain machine-visible.

## Artifact grammar

Every artifact is defined by ten fields:

> **Artifact = Origin × Form × Substrate × Resonance × Pattern × Grant × Bond × Price × Boundary × Memory**

| Field | Governing question |
|---|---|
| **Origin** | Who or what made it, and what historical event made that possible? |
| **Form** | Why does this capability require this physical or living interface? |
| **Substrate** | Which Arcanean material carries the resonance? |
| **Resonance** | Which Elements, Expressions, Gates, and frequencies can it conduct? |
| **Pattern** | Which Attack, Defense, or Summoning technique does it instantiate? |
| **Grant** | Does it focus, unlock, store, bridge, pact, engine, seal, or assemble? |
| **Bond** | What recognizes a bearer: touch, oath, lineage, consent, community, or world? |
| **Price** | What is spent: mana, time, vitality, memory, identity, oath, material, relationship, ecology, or attention? |
| **Boundary** | What can it never do, and how can an opponent stop it? |
| **Memory** | What does it remember, and how does use alter its future behavior? |

An artifact record without a price, boundary, counterplay, and consequence is incomplete regardless of prose quality.

## Artifact classes

| Class | Function | Hard limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Focus** | amplifies an ability already available to the bearer | cannot create missing affinity |
| **Key** | unlocks one bounded technique while attuned | does not open the corresponding Gate |
| **Vessel** | stores energy, memory, a spell, or a summoned pattern | capacity is finite and inspectable |
| **Bridge** | stabilizes a derived Expression across Elements | cannot erase component incompatibility |
| **Pact** | shares capability with an agentive being or living artifact | the other party retains consent and agenda |
| **Engine** | performs collective, architectural, vehicle, or landscape-scale work | requires infrastructure and produces externalities |
| **Seal** | suppresses, contains, redirects, or counters magic | suppression affects allies and bearer too |
| **Assembly** | composes several artifacts into a higher-order working | weakest component and highest Gate requirement govern |

## Access, skill, and Gate Debt

Artifact access has four separable dimensions:

1. **Affinity** — what the bearer naturally conducts.
2. **Access** — what an artifact makes invocable.
3. **Control** — what training allows the bearer to shape safely.
4. **Authority** — what opened Gates and rank permit the bearer to sustain.

A Key can supply access without affinity. It cannot supply control or authority.

When a bearer invokes an artifact above their sustainable authority, the artifact either refuses or creates **Gate Debt**: temporary resonance borrowed against the bearer's body, memory, relationships, or environment. Gate Debt never counts as an opened Gate and cannot produce rank. Unpaid Gate Debt becomes fracture, mutation, possession, ecological damage, or Shadow vulnerability according to the artifact record.

Permanent affinity imprinting is exceptional. It requires explicit consent, a transformative capability, Sacred-or-higher gating, irreversible cost, and a world profile that permits it. No portable object may mass-imprint affinity by default.

## Combination law

Artifacts combine through named roles rather than arbitrary stacking:

- **Core** — defines the primary capability.
- **Frame** — determines physical scope and durability.
- **Catalyst** — accelerates or raises output while increasing price.
- **Ward** — contains failure and creates counterplay.
- **Keystone** — synchronizes the assembly and determines its Gate requirement.

Combination rules:

1. One assembly has exactly one Core and no more than one Keystone.
2. Its effective Gate is the highest component Gate plus one for each unresolved elemental incompatibility, capped at ten.
3. Its effective tier follows the canonical Gate-to-tier mapping; components cannot vote power upward.
4. A derived Expression requires every component Element or a documented substitution with an explicit loss.
5. Two prices never cancel. They compound or transform into a more consequential price.
6. Any component with agency can refuse the assembly.
7. Destroying, separating, silencing, or detuning the Keystone is valid counterplay.

## Concordance

**Concordance** is a temporary attainment state, not an origin class, rank, office, reincarnation cycle, or singular chosen person.

A bearer enters Concordance when multiple resonances remain mutually legible without one consuming the others. The state requires adequate Gates, a compatible Assembly, embodied control, and a price paid in real time.

The proposed apex Assembly, **the Tenfold Consonance**, can be stabilized only by a complete Luminor. It does not make a Luminor. It allows one brief synthesis across the Five Elements and their Spirit/Void duality; after use, the most imbalanced Gate closes to the Assembly until restored through story-level consequence.

This is Arcanea's answer to the multi-element sovereign archetype: earned harmonic responsibility, not exclusive birthright and not unrestricted omnipotence.

## Many worlds, one cosmology

Arcanea is not one uniform planet. It is a multiverse with a shared cosmic kernel and world-local manifestations.

Every World Profile inherits the locked core and may vary:

- which derived Expressions are known or culturally named;
- where materials occur and how artifacts are manufactured;
- whether artifact access is sacred, regulated, commercialized, inherited, or ubiquitous;
- which costs societies normalize or forbid;
- how portals and cross-world artifacts behave;
- which factions control knowledge, repair, licensing, or interdiction.

A World Profile may not vary Elements, the Lumina/Nero relationship, Shadow's corruption status, the Ten Gates, Guardians, ranks, disciplines, or tiers.

Canonicality is scoped:

| Scope | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **core** | binding across Arcanea after explicit approval |
| **world** | binding only inside one approved world |
| **story** | true for one continuity or authored work |
| **community** | remixable contribution, not official canon |
| **experimental** | design-space exploration; excluded from default retrieval |

## Darkenia — working World Profile

**Darkenia** is a STAGING world name and a stress test for the system.

Its premise: an artifact civilization democratized magical access faster than it could distribute discipline, context, and responsibility. Power became portable; culture did not. Cities now compete over resonance mines, living Engines, relic repair, and the debts left by generations who treated borrowed capability as mastery.

Darkenia is dark because of institutions, history, scarcity, Malachar's Shadow contamination, and human choices—not because Nero or Void are evil.

World rules:

- Keys and Vessels are common; Bridges and Engines define geopolitical power.
- Gate Debt is legal, financialized, inherited, and therefore politically explosive.
- Verdance is a major force: living artifacts can heal damaged lands, but only through consent and reciprocal ecology.
- Shadow artifacts counterfeit resonance by severing Spirit from Void; they are quarantined, trafficked, and studied under ward.
- The central conflict is not “who has magic?” but “who controls the interfaces, repairs the costs, and remembers what every artifact did?”

This makes Darkenia the ideal first world for artifact quests, factions, found relics, combinatorial builds, ethical power transfer, and community-created objects.

## Community contribution contract

Every community artifact moves through:

```text
experimental record
  -> schema validation
  -> canon and similarity checks
  -> world-scoped staging
  -> human review
  -> approved Git revision
  -> signed Starlight Memory projection
  -> encyclopedia index
```

Submission requires:

- a stable ID and world scope;
- one primary capability and explicit secondary effects;
- canonical Element, discipline, tier, Gate, and rank fields;
- substrate, provenance, price, constraints, failure modes, and counterplay;
- narrative desire, temptation, fear, and historical consequence;
- influence IDs plus an originality statement;
- creator attribution, license, remix terms, and version.

Default encyclopedia queries return approved records only. STAGING and experimental records require an explicit filter and visible status treatment.

## Memory and encyclopedia boundary

Git is the authority. Starlight Memory is a signed, rebuildable projection optimized for retrieval and relationship traversal.

Each projected record carries its entity ID, world, scope, status, version, content hash, source commit, provenance, rights risk, and relations. Memory may suggest conflicts or related entities; it may never promote, rewrite, or merge canon. A projection that disagrees with Git is discarded and rebuilt.

## Spirit interoperability

The STAGING Spirit & Archetype System in SPIRIT_ARCHETYPE_SYSTEM.md adds personhood, ecology, and relationship rules to artifact interfaces.

- Uppercase Spirit remains the locked Element; lowercase spirit identifies a being or presence.
- An artifact can witness, invite, house, translate, channel, seal, or co-steward a relationship.
- Pact-class artifacts authenticate a bounded Accord. A name never grants ownership or command.
- A spirit-mediated capability remains Gate-bound and never grants rank, opened Gates, cultural mastery, or the spirit's full authority.
- Agentive spirits and artifacts retain refusal, renegotiation, transfer, repair, and release rights.
- Spirit–artifact bonds are separate records so either endpoint can evolve without silently rewriting the other.
- Predatory binding, identity severance, involuntary imprisonment, and Spirit-stripped Shadow interfaces are quarantined.

The crosswalk is defined in magic-intelligence-system/docs/SPIRIT-ARTIFACT-CROSSWALK.md. Original spirit records, comparative research, and projection rules live in spirit-intelligence-system. Both systems inherit the same Git-authoritative canon boundary.
