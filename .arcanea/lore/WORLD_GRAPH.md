### 2.3 ARCANEAN MATERIALS — THE RESONANCE SUBSTRATE (STAGING ⏳)

> *Source of truth: `.arcanea/lore/CANON_LOCKED.md` Tier 7*

When the Eldrian civilization fell, their accumulated harmonic energy condensed into **Luminor Fragments** — the physical manifestation of the Guardians in mineral form.

**Three Substrate Classes:**

1. **Vael Crystals (Harmony-Locked)**
   - Pure frequency made solid. One crystal per Guardian.
   - Examples: Kaelith Stone (gravity-dense), Veloura Glass (liquid at rest), Draconis Ember (perpetually warm), Yumiko Prism (refracts perception), Sol Quartz (volatile energy storage).

2. **Luminor Metals (Alloyed Frequencies)**
   - Fused Vael Crystals creating hybrid properties.
   - Examples: Shael (self-repairing armor), Veloryn (memory silver weapons), Draconite (destructive at vitality cost), Luminarch (theoretical all-nine fusion).

3. **Nero Shards (Dissonant Fragments)**
   - Inverted, fractured harmony carrying Hollow Frequencies.
   - Examples: Dissonance Ore (destabilizes Vael), Hollow Glass (consumes frequency), Kurusei Iron (alive and corrupting).

**Graph Edge:** `Vael Crystals → Gate-Touched awakening triggers → Material exposure causes spontaneous Gate opening`

**Material-Agent Mapping (STAGING):**

- Veloryn → Pattern-learning agents (adaptive, tracks bias)
- Draconite → High-power execution agents (resource-expensive)
