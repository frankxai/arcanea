# Arcanea Copy

Arcanea copy should feel like invitation plus mechanism.

## Reader and decision

Write for creators, worldbuilders, artists, young co-creators, and readers drawn to myth, music, and imagination. The reader should feel the world close enough to enter through one creative act.

## Rules

- Name the world or ritual, then make the action clear.
- Use sensory words when they reveal meaning.
- Keep product instructions plain.
- Protect comprehension: never let lore hide the button, price, next step, or outcome.
- Use "chosen by responsibility" only when the copy immediately gives the user a concrete trial, proof artifact, or stewardship action.
- Use "The Drift" for system failures: passive consumption, synthetic confusion, creative amnesia, isolation, rights fog, and tool dependency.
- Never frame people, countries, religions, cultures, or political outgroups as the enemy.
- Anchor every passage in a scene, character, object, or creative act.
- Give every lore term a plain meaning on first use.
- Use light, ocean, stars, sound, and elemental imagery with restraint.

## Bad

"Enter the infinite realm of limitless creation."

## Better

"Open the Forge, choose a Luminor path, and turn one idea into a finished creative asset."

## Avoid

- Lore dumps and adjective chains.
- Generic fantasy language and synthetic spirituality.
- Endless invented nouns.
- Trailer slogans that carry no scene or consequence.

## Chosen Responsibility Example

Bad:

"You are the chosen one. Join us against the enemy."

Better:

"Choose the part of The Drift you will answer. Arcanea turns that call into one Gift, one Trial, and one proof artifact you can keep."
